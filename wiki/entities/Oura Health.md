# Oura Health

Finnish wearables company making the Oura Ring, a continuous health-monitoring smart ring. As of 2024-2025, Oura Gen3 with OSSA 2.0 is the most independently validated consumer device for sleep staging accuracy.

## Products

**Oura Ring Gen3 + OSSA 2.0** (2023-2025):
- PPG-based HRV, resting heart rate, body temperature, blood oxygen
- Sleep staging, readiness score, activity tracking, menstrual cycle tracking
- Form factor: titanium ring with 7-day battery life

**Oura Ring Gen4** (2024-2025):
- Improved CCC 0.98/0.99 for resting HR/HRV vs ECG reference (Dial et al., Physiological Reports 2025; n=13, 536 nights)

## Validation Data

| Study | Metric | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Chee et al., Sleep Medicine 2024 | Epoch-level accuracy vs PSG | 91.7-91.8% |
| Chee et al. 2024 | Sleep-vs-wake sensitivity | 94.4-94.5% |
| Chee et al. 2024 | PABAK | 0.83-0.84 |
| Chee et al. 2024 | Study size | 96 participants, 421,045 epochs |
| Robbins et al. (Sensors 24(20):6532, 2024) | Four-stage classification ranking | Best of Oura/Fitbit/Apple Watch |
| Dial et al. (Physiological Reports, 2025) | Nocturnal RHR/HRV CCC (Gen4) | 0.98/0.99 |

> [!key-insight] Best-validated sleep ring
> Two independent studies (Chee 2024 + Brigham & Women's 2024) rank Oura Gen3 as the most accurate consumer device for multi-stage sleep classification. However, the six-device Schyvens 2025 study finds specificity 29.4-52.2% across ALL consumer devices including Oura, indicating systematic over-calling of sleep.

## Research Partnerships

Oura collaborates with Duke University, Singapore National University, Brigham & Women's Hospital, UNC Chapel Hill, and Google Fitbit on research studies. This academic network is a competitive differentiator vs. more closed ecosystems.

## Market Position

- Smart ring category: 1.66M units shipped in 2024 (Statista).
- Samsung Galaxy Ring (Feb 2024) is the primary new competitor.
- Oura pioneered the screen-less recovery-band form factor (similar positioning to Whoop but ring format).

## Use Cases

- Sleep optimization, menstrual cycle tracking, COVID-19 illness prediction (early pandemic studies), long-COVID monitoring
- Research tool: published in peer-reviewed journals; Oura API available for researchers
